Why Your PT Website Needs a Complete Overhaul

Many of our clients come to us because they’re not seeing enough conversions. These are the most common issues we find and work through one by one.

01

No Mention of Direct Access to PT

Since patients in most states can see a physical therapist without a doctor’s referral, if you don’t make that clear, visitors may think they need to see their doctor first and never contact you.

02

Insurance Information Can’t Be Found Easily

It’s super important for patients to know if you’ll take their insurance before they decide to reach out to you. So, make sure your insurance information is right in front of their eyes.

03

No Patient-Centric Design

Does your site only have an “About Us” page and a list of treatment options? However, in reality, patients mainly care about whether you’re the right professional to help with their conditions.

04

No Result-Focused Content

That means you’re always talking about techniques instead of outcomes. You should also mention how exactly your treatment can help them in their everyday lives.

05

The User Flow Doesn’t Cater to Different Types of Patients

You can call your site structure great when it gives every major type of patient a tailored journey that fits their concerns and expectations. Pixxen excels at this approach.

06

You Have a Common “Services” Page

We’re so tired of this age-old issue! You need a dedicated page for every major therapy you provide so you can build a specific SEO strategy for each one.

07

No Dedicated Location Pages

The same rule applies here. You’re a local business, so you should have dedicated pages for major areas (like a city or metro) and mention smaller neighborhoods throughout the website.

08

You Don’t Look So Credible

Those in pain want to feel confident that they’re choosing real PT experts in their locality. Your site should have the right trust signals to reinforce that.

09

Slow Follow-up After an Inquiry

When a patient fills out a form or requests an appointment, your team should get back to them immediately. You’ll lose precious leads if that inquiry takes too long to follow up.

9 features PT clinic owners actually use (and most websites skip)

Every feature on this list exists because we've seen it move the needle on booked leads. Nothing here is decoration.

Insurance Accepted Prominently Displayed

BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, Medicaid, workers' comp carriers — visible in the header and on every service page. Filters insurance-shoppers before the call.

Direct-access messaging where state law allows

Visible 'You don't need a doctor's referral' messaging for direct-access states. Wins the self-referring patient who'd otherwise assume they need a physician visit first.

Condition-specific landing pages

Lower back pain, ACL recovery, rotator cuff, vestibular, pelvic floor, post-stroke, frozen shoulder — each gets its own page targeting that specific search intent.

Therapist profiles with certifications

DPT, OCS, SCS, GCS, OMPT, manual therapy, dry needling — credentials displayed prominently so condition-matching is obvious to prospects.

Online appointment booking integrated

WebPT, Prompt, HENO, or your EMR — bookings route directly into your scheduling system with insurance verification at intake.

Outcome content for common conditions

Recovery timelines, what to expect, exercises and education content. Long-tail SEO content that captures research-stage patients.

CallRail with text-back

Consultation forms trigger instant text-back to the patient confirming receipt and offering next available appointment slots. Cuts response time from 24 hours to under 10 minutes.

Telehealth landing page with hybrid messaging

Visible hybrid in-person + telehealth option for patients who prefer flexibility. Captures the 15–25% post-COVID preference for remote-first care.

Why can’t you create or claim my Google Business Profile?

That’s because Google usually takes 7-14 days to verify a profile. Since we’re building your whole website within a short timeline, we can’t afford to wait that long for the verification process.

We don’t store protected health information (PHI) on your website. Rather, we use your existing EMR or a HIPAA-compliant form solution when sensitive patient information is required. We also make sure your site can safely take care of all information.
