Why Physical Therapy Websites Lose Patients Before Booking

Independent PT clinics compete against ATI Physical Therapy, Select Physical Therapy, and dozens of franchises with national SEO budgets. Most independent PT sites we audit are leaking qualified first-visit bookings.

01

No direct-access information visible

49 states allow direct PT access without a referral. Sites that hide this can lose self-referring patients.

02

Insurance accepted not listed

Patients filter by insurance before they call. Sites without visible BCBS, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and major workers' comp carriers lose qualified leads to clinics that publish them.

03

Generic services page, not condition-specific

Lower back pain, ACL recovery, frozen shoulder, vestibular rehab, post-stroke - each is a distinct search intent with different urgency. One page ranks for none.

04

No therapist profiles with specialties

Orthopedic Certified Specialist (OCS), Sports Certified Specialist (SCS), Geriatric Certified (GCS), Manual Therapy certifications - patients with specific conditions look for matching specialists.

05

Online appointment booking missing or broken

Patients in pain want to book at 9pm, not call tomorrow. Sites without integrated booking via WebPT, Prompt, or HENO route bookings through the front desk and lose 30-40% of after-hours intent.

06

No condition-outcome content

'How long does PT take for ACL recovery?' 'What to expect after rotator cuff surgery?' - patients research outcomes before booking. Sites that answer these win the research-stage patient.

07

Slow consultation response time

Internal data: PT prospects who don't hear back within 4 hours convert at 1/3 the rate. CallRail with text-back integration cuts response time and saves the lead.

08

Telehealth offering hidden

Post-COVID, 15-25% of PT prospects prefer hybrid in-person/telehealth. Sites without telehealth visibility lose this segment to clinics that surface it.

09

No referral form for physicians

Orthopedists, primary care, and surgeons refer patients. Sites without a quick physician referral form lose institutional partnerships to clinics that make referral frictionless.
